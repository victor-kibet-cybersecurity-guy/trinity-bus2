Cross-border travel guide

Passport and travel document planning for cross-border bus trips

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Carry the original travel document accepted for your nationality and itinerary. A national ID may be accepted on some regional journeys, but eligibility depends on citizenship and current government rules.

Before you travel

  • Check document validity and condition.
  • Confirm visa or entry-authorisation requirements.
  • Keep documents in hand luggage.
  • Carry any required health certificates.
Requirements can change. Confirm current rules with official immigration authorities before departure.
