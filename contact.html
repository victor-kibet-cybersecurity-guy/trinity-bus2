Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly does the WhatsApp desk respond?

Inquiries sent during operating hours (5:30 AM – 10:00 PM EAT) are typically acknowledged within 15–30 minutes.

Can I send parcels via Trinity Bus Express?

Yes. Small packages, luggage transfers, and commercial parcels can be booked at any terminal office prior to coach departure.
