Frequently Asked Questions

Booking, cross-border travel, luggage, changes, refunds, safety and parcel enquiries.

How do I request a booking?

Choose a route, complete the booking form and send the generated WhatsApp message.

When is my booking confirmed?

Only after Trinity Bus Express verifies seat availability and payment.

Are website fares final?

No. Fares are estimates and are subject to change until verified.

Does the website accept payment?

No. The website does not process online payments.

Do I need an account?

No account or login is required.

Can I book by phone?

Yes. Call +254 754 932 823.

Can I book through WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the published WhatsApp number +254 754 932 823.

Can I choose a seat?

You can request preferred seats in the booking interface. Final seat assignment requires verification.

Why does the site show available seats?

The frontend uses editable planning data. It is not live database inventory.

How early should I report?

Use the reporting time supplied during booking and allow extra time for international journeys.

What passport do I need?

Use a valid travel document accepted for your itinerary and verify requirements with official authorities.

Do I need a visa?

Visa or entry requirements depend on nationality, destination and travel purpose. Verify with official authorities.

Are health documents required?

Requirements vary. Check current official health and entry guidance before travel.

How long do border crossings take?

Processing time varies with queues, checks and operational conditions.

What luggage is allowed?

Confirm the current luggage allowance during booking because route and coach conditions can vary.

Can I carry valuables in checked luggage?

Keep travel documents, money, medicine and important valuables in hand luggage under your control.

Can children travel?

Ask reservations about child fares, documents, seating and guardian requirements for your itinerary.

Are pets allowed?

Contact reservations before travel because animal transport rules and border requirements vary.

Can I change my travel date?

Contact Trinity Bus Express as early as possible. Changes depend on availability and the applicable booking terms.

Can I cancel a booking?

Contact the company promptly. Cancellation and refund handling depends on the verified booking arrangement.

How are refunds handled?

Read the refund policy and contact the team with your booking reference and verified payment details.

Can I send a parcel?

Contact Trinity Bus Express to confirm route, item acceptance, packaging, pricing and collection procedure.

Can I track a parcel online?

This frontend does not include a parcel-tracking database.

What happens if the bus is delayed?

Road, border, traffic and operational conditions can change arrival times. Contact the team for current trip information.

Are departure points fixed?

Confirm the exact reporting point during booking because operational locations can change.

Are arrival points fixed?

Confirm the current arrival point and local connection plan before departure.

What should I pack for a long trip?

Carry documents, water where permitted, medication, charging options, warm layers and basic personal items.

Can I make a tight onward connection?

Avoid relying on an estimated international bus arrival for a tight onward connection.

Which countries appear in the travel guides?

Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, South Sudan, DRC and Burundi.

Which routes are listed?

The site lists eight core city-pair routes in the Routes section.

How do I report an emergency?

Use local emergency services where appropriate and contact Trinity Bus Express through the published phone number for trip-related support.

How do I verify a payment request?

Call the published Trinity Bus Express phone number before paying if you are uncertain about any instruction.
