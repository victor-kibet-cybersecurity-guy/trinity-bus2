Accessibility information

Passengers who use wheelchairs, walking aids or require boarding assistance should contact Trinity Bus Express before booking. Public fleet information reviewed for this page does not establish a consistent wheelchair lift, low-floor entrance or dedicated wheelchair space across the entire fleet.

When contacting the operator, provide your route, date, mobility needs, mobility-device dimensions where relevant and the type of assistance required.

Are Trinity buses wheelchair accessible?
