Passenger safety and travel preparation

Passenger Safety & Travel Guidance

Use this page to prepare for regional and cross-border travel. It covers practical safety steps for drivers, vehicles, luggage, night travel, border crossings, emergencies and lost property.

Read Safety Guide Contact Support

During travel Keep valuables secure, follow crew instructions and stay aware at stops.
Cross-border journeys Check official immigration and health requirements before travelling.
01

Driver standards

Professional bus operations should use properly licensed drivers who meet the legal requirements for the vehicle class and route they operate.

  • Drivers should hold the correct valid licence for the vehicle category.
  • Drivers should follow road traffic laws, speed limits and operator procedures.
  • Cross-border drivers should understand the routes, terminals and border procedures used on their service.
  • Operators should manage driving hours and rest periods in line with applicable law and internal safety procedures.
  • Passengers should report unsafe driving to the operator as soon as it is safe to do so.
02

Vehicle inspection

Roadworthy buses depend on routine checks and scheduled maintenance. Passengers should never rely on appearance alone as proof of mechanical condition.

  • Tyres should have suitable tread and inflation.
  • Lights, indicators, mirrors and windscreen equipment should function correctly.
  • Brakes, steering and suspension should receive scheduled inspection and maintenance.
  • Doors, emergency exits and luggage compartments should operate correctly.
  • Visible defects should be reported before departure.
03

Emergency equipment

Passenger vehicles should carry the emergency equipment required by applicable law and operator policy.

  • Emergency exits should remain accessible.
  • Fire extinguishers should be present where required and kept serviceable.
  • First-aid supplies should be available where required.
  • Reflective warning equipment and other roadside safety items should be carried where required.
  • Passengers should identify the nearest emergency exit after boarding.
04

Passenger conduct

Safe travel also depends on passenger behaviour. Follow crew instructions and avoid actions that distract the driver or create risks for other passengers.

  • Remain seated while the bus is moving where possible.
  • Keep aisles and emergency exits clear.
  • Do not interfere with vehicle controls, doors or safety equipment.
  • Do not carry prohibited, hazardous or illegal items.
  • Treat other passengers, staff and border officials respectfully.
05

Luggage security

Keep important documents, money, phones and other valuables with you rather than inside unattended checked luggage.

  • Label your luggage with your name and a reachable contact number.
  • Use secure zips or locks where appropriate.
  • Do not leave passports, cash or medication in checked luggage.
  • Check your bag before leaving every stop or terminal.
  • Keep baggage receipts or tags when the operator issues them.
06

Night travel

Night routes require extra preparation because boarding, stopovers and arrival may happen when fewer services are open.

  • Confirm the exact boarding point before leaving for the terminal.
  • Arrive early enough to avoid rushing.
  • Keep your phone charged and carry a power bank if needed.
  • Share your travel details with a trusted person.
  • Arrange safe onward transport if your destination arrival is late at night or early in the morning.
07

Border procedures

Immigration, visa, health and customs requirements depend on your nationality, destination and current government rules.

  • Carry valid travel identification and any required visa or permit.
  • Check passport validity requirements before departure.
  • Follow immigration and customs instructions at every border point.
  • Do not carry restricted goods across borders.
  • Use official government sources to verify current entry and health rules.
Do not rely on old social media posts or screenshots for immigration requirements. Rules change.
08

Emergency contacts

Save the operator's current support number before departure and keep local emergency-service information for the countries on your route.

  • Save Trinity Bus Express support details from the Contact page.
  • Keep your booking reference or travel details accessible.
  • In a serious emergency, contact local emergency services or police first where appropriate.
  • Provide your bus route, current location and direction of travel when reporting an incident.
Do not publish emergency numbers on this page unless you have verified they are current for each country served.
09

Lost property

If you leave an item on a bus, at a terminal or during a stopover, report it as soon as possible.

  • Provide your name and contact details.
  • Give the route, travel date and approximate departure time.
  • Describe the item clearly, including colour, brand or identifying marks.
  • Provide your seat number or booking reference if available.
  • Never send passwords, PINs or other sensitive account information as proof of ownership.
10

Before you travel

A short preparation check reduces avoidable problems on long-distance routes.

  • Confirm the departure date and reporting time.
  • Confirm the boarding terminal or pick-up point.
  • Check current fare and payment instructions.
  • Verify travel documents for cross-border routes.
  • Carry drinking water, essential medication and a charged phone where appropriate.

Passenger safety FAQ

What should I do if I notice unsafe driving?

Avoid confronting the driver while the vehicle is moving. Report the concern to the operator as soon as it is safe, with the route, date, time and any useful identifying details.

Where should I keep my passport and money?

Keep important documents, money, medication and small valuables with you rather than in unattended checked luggage.

What should I do before travelling at night?

Confirm your terminal, reporting time and onward transport, keep your phone charged and share your travel details with a trusted person.

How should I confirm border requirements?

Use current official immigration, customs and health sources for the countries on your route. Requirements differ by nationality and change over time.

What information should I provide for lost property?

Provide the route, date, approximate travel time, seat or booking details and a clear description of the missing item.

Need help before your journey?

Confirm the latest terminal, departure time, fare, luggage information and route details before travelling.
