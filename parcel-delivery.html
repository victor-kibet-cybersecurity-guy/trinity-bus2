What to prepare

  • Sender name and phone number
  • Recipient name and phone number
  • Destination town
  • Clear parcel description
  • Approximate size and weight
  • Secure packaging

Before sending

  • Confirm the parcel is accepted
  • Confirm the current charge
  • Confirm the departure terminal
  • Confirm the destination collection point
  • Ask what identification the recipient needs
  • Keep any receipt or reference issued

Cross-border parcels

  • Describe contents accurately
  • Do not send prohibited or illegal goods
  • Check customs requirements where applicable
  • Keep invoices or supporting documents when required
  • Confirm whether duties or taxes apply
  • Follow official border and customs rules

Parcel delivery FAQ

How much does Nairobi to Kampala parcel delivery cost?

The rate depends on current operator pricing and parcel details such as size, weight and item type. Request a confirmed quote before sending.

How do I send a parcel from Kenya to Uganda?

Confirm the route and parcel requirements, prepare and label the item, take it to the confirmed sending point and keep any receipt or parcel reference issued.

Where do I drop off a parcel in Nairobi?

Confirm the current Trinity Bus Express parcel drop-off terminal before travelling because operational locations can change.

Where does the recipient collect the parcel in Kampala?

Ask the parcel desk to confirm the current Kampala collection point and the identification or reference required for collection.

How long does Nairobi to Kampala parcel delivery take?

Timing depends on dispatch availability, route conditions, border procedures and the current operating schedule. Request the latest expected dispatch and collection window.

What items can I send?

Accepted-item rules vary. Describe the parcel to the operator before sending and ask whether the item is permitted.

Can I send business goods from Kenya to Uganda?

Some commercial goods may involve customs declarations, invoices, duties or other requirements. Confirm both operator acceptance and official customs requirements before dispatch.

How do I track my parcel?

Use the parcel receipt, booking reference or contact process provided by Trinity Bus Express when the parcel is accepted.
