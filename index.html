Cross-Border Coach Travel Across East Africa

Trinity Bus Express offers reliable long-distance coach travel information connecting major East African urban hubs, including Nairobi, Kampala, Kigali, Juba, Goma, and Bujumbura. Passengers can browse route schedules, compare fare estimates, review reporting procedures, and submit seat reservation requests via WhatsApp. Every booking request is verified by office staff to ensure accurate seat availability, updated departure schedules, and clear M-Pesa or cash payment instructions.

Booking Through WhatsApp & Terminal Offices

To preserve passenger data security and ensure flexibility without online processing fees, Trinity Bus Express operates a WhatsApp-first reservation workflow. When you select a route, specify travel dates, and choose preferred seats on our interactive coach layout, a structured WhatsApp request is generated containing a unique booking reference code. Upon sending, our desk staff reviews seat availability and confirms your reporting time at our River Road, Bakuli, or Nyabugogo terminal offices.

Border Crossings & Document Preparation in 2026

International road journeys require proper preparation for border posts such as Busia / Malaba (Kenya-Uganda), Gatuna / Katuna (Uganda-Rwanda), Elegu / Nimule (Uganda-South Sudan), and Grande BarriÃƒÂ¨re (Rwanda-DRC). Passengers must ensure their passports have at least 6 months validity, present Yellow Fever vaccination cards where required, and carry relevant visas or East Africa Tourist Visa permits. Because border processing times fluctuate, published journey durations are estimates and passengers are advised to avoid tight onward connections immediately after border crossings.

Coach Amenities & On-Board Comfort

Long-distance travel is made comfortable with our fleet of executive coaches featuring 2+2 luxury reclining seating, USB charging ports, overhead hand luggage racks, and spacious under-floor baggage compartments. Each ticket includes one main luggage piece up to 30kg plus one personal item inside the coach. Passengers are advised to keep passports, medicines, phones, and valuables in a small hand carry bag throughout the journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a seat with Trinity Bus Express?

Choose your route on our booking page, pick your travel date, select your preferred seats on the coach map, and click "Send Booking Request on WhatsApp". Our office staff will verify seat availability and provide payment details to confirm your seat.

Are ticket prices shown on the website guaranteed?

Prices listed are current estimated route fares. Final prices are confirmed by our booking office when your WhatsApp reservation is verified.

How early should I arrive at the departure terminal?

All passengers must report at least 45 minutes prior to departure at the designated terminal office for luggage tagging, document verification, and boarding clearance.

Does Trinity Bus Express collect online card payments?

No. This website does not collect credit card or bank details. Payment is arranged via official mobile money merchant shortcodes (M-Pesa) or directly at our booking offices.
